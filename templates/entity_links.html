🛡️ Privacy by design

This analysis uses only data already stored with each claim (invoices, document fingerprints, serial numbers, owners, retailer-price combinations). No EXIF/GPS extraction, no device fingerprinting, no new data collection — privacy constraints are honored by construction.

🔎 How it works: every claim's linkable entities are extracted and compared across the database. Claims sharing entities are linked; clusters of 3+ claims from multiple owners sharing 2+ entity types are flagged as potential fraud rings and escalated to priority review. The syndicate_min_cluster setting in config/settings.yaml controls sensitivity.
