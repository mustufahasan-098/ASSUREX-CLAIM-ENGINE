AssureX Claim Engine
AI Usage Disclosure
Overview

Artificial intelligence tools were utilized during the development of the AssureX Claim Engine as supporting development resources. Their use was limited to specific development and content-generation tasks and did not replace the overall system design, implementation, testing, or decision-making performed by the development team.

1. GLM

Tool: GLM
Primary Use: Software Development Assistance

GLM was used as a coding assistance tool during the development and debugging of the AssureX Claim Engine. Its primary applications included:

Identifying programming errors and potential causes.
Assisting with debugging and troubleshooting.
Suggesting code-level fixes for identified issues.
Reviewing problematic sections of code and proposing improvements.
Assisting in understanding runtime errors and implementation issues.
Supporting code refinement during development.

Any suggested code or solutions were reviewed and adapted as necessary before being incorporated into the project. GLM was used strictly as a development aid and not as an autonomous decision-making component of the AssureX claim-processing system.

2. Google AI Studio

Tool: Google AI Studio
Primary Use: Sample Image Generation

Google AI Studio was used to generate sample product images for development, testing, and demonstration purposes.

The generated images were used to:

Provide representative product examples within the application.
Support testing of product-related workflows.
Demonstrate the functionality of the AssureX Claim Engine.
Provide visual assets for project demonstrations and presentations.

These images are synthetic sample assets and do not represent real customer products, insurance documents, or authentic claims.

Statement of Responsibility

AI tools were used as supplementary resources during development. The project architecture, implementation, integration, testing, validation, and final development decisions remained under the responsibility of the project developer(s).

The use of AI-generated assistance does not imply that the AI tools independently developed or operated the AssureX Claim Engine.
